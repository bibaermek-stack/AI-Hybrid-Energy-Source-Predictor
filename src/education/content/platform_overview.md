# EcoPredict AI — Smart Educational Platform

**Theme:** Artificial Intelligence-Driven Optimization of Renewable Energy Systems.

## Learning modules

1. **Forecasting** — Random Forest / XGBoost solar–wind prediction from weather features  
2. **Fault detection** — YOLOv11 thermal/panel defect detection + CNN clean/dirty  
3. **Optimization** — PuLP multi-hour hybrid dispatch (solar + wind + battery + grid)  
4. **AI Advisor (RAG)** — Kazakh / English knowledge base (ChromaDB)  
5. **Sustainability** — CO₂, LCOE, ROI, payback for Kazakhstan contexts  

## Site focus

Default demo site: **Turkistan, Kazakhstan** (dust, high temperature, grid carbon intensity).

## How to use this platform

1. Open **Predictions** for single-step hybrid dispatch.  
2. Open **Forecast** for 24 h weather-driven generation.  
3. Open **Optimization** for multi-hour battery LP schedules.  
4. Open **Learn & Explore** for lessons, labs, and quizzes.  
5. Open **Sustainability** for carbon and economic impact.
