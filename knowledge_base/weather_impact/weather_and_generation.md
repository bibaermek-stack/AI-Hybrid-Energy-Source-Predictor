# Weather impact on solar generation

## Irradiance
Plane-of-array irradiance (W/m²) is the main driver of DC power. Clear sky peak near solar noon; heavy cloud can cut power by 50–90%.

## Temperature
Module temperature above 25°C reduces efficiency (~0.3–0.5%/°C for crystalline Si). Hot dry days: high irradiance but lower voltage and efficiency.

## Cloud cover
Rapid cloud edges cause ramps. EcoPredict 24h forecast uses weather API cloud and temperature for Turkistan to estimate generation.

## Wind and cooling
Light wind cools modules and can slightly improve efficiency. Strong storms increase soiling after dust events.

## Seasonality (general)
- Summer: higher sun angles and longer days; more soiling and heat derating.
- Winter: lower sun, shorter days; snow/ice rare in south Kazakhstan but fog/haze possible.

## Hybrid note
Wind often stronger at night or winter; solar by day/summer. Hybrid dispatch balances both sources.
