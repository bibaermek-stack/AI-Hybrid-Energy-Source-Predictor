# Kazakhstan / Turkistan solar context

## Climate
Southern Kazakhstan (Turkistan region) has high summer irradiance, low humidity, and frequent dust — good resource, higher soiling losses.

## Grid
Inverters must match local grid voltage/frequency settings. Frequent voltage excursions can cause disconnects; check Solarman grid status and event logs.

## Tariff and ROI (EcoPredict)
Economics tools use CAPEX, OPEX, and KZT electricity tariff with inflation. Payback depends on self-consumption vs export rules at the site.

## Local operations
- Prefer morning cleaning schedules in heat season.
- Plan maintenance outside peak generation hours (11:00–15:00).
- Monitor WeatherAPI-based forecasts for cloudy days and adjust load or storage.

## Language
EcoPredict UI supports Kazakh, English, and Russian for operators on site.
