# Panel cleaning guide

## When to clean
- After dust storms or long dry periods.
- When specific yield (kWh/kWp) drops without matching weather (high irradiance, low AC power).
- Monthly visual check is recommended in Turkistan / southern Kazakhstan summers.

## How to clean
1. Prefer early morning or evening (cool glass — less thermal shock).
2. Use clean water and soft brushes or approved solar cleaning tools.
3. Avoid abrasive pads and harsh detergents that etch anti-reflective coating.
4. Do not walk on panels; use safe walkways and fall protection on roofs.

## Water quality
Hard water can leave mineral film. Prefer demineralized or filtered water when possible.

## Safety
De-energize work near exposed DC if required by local rules. Never clean during storms or high winds.

## After cleaning
Expect a step-up in AC power under similar irradiance within minutes to hours. Log pre/post power in EcoPredict for PR tracking.
